## What it is

A blockchain and AI education platform that unites learning, events and careers in one hub. Members
take interactive modules with timed exams, attend workshops and meetups, and apply to Web3 gigs.
Progress rolls up into BLX points, badges, certificates and a shareable public profile.

## Value delivered

- Learners turn scattered self-study into a guided path with verifiable proof of skill —
  certificates and a shareable digital resume.
- Employers get one funnel from education to hiring: a gig board ranking candidates by what they
  actually completed.
- Operators generate course content with AI, scaling the catalog without scaling headcount.

## Technology highlights

- **AI module generation** — Anthropic Claude via LangChain.js drafts full lessons and exams from a
  topic prompt.
- **Web3-native rewards and identity** — wagmi/viem plus Coinbase CDP power the BLX economy,
  certificates and QR check-ins.
